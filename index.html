Step 1 · Find

What do you do over and over?

8 minutes

Open a normal new chat. Fill in the two boxes, then copy and paste. No job of your own? Skip the boxes and borrow one below.

I'm not technical. I run a small business in Petaling Jaya. Ask me one at a time what jobs I do every week, over and over, and help me put a rough time on each. Use plain words. Don't suggest any tool yet.

At work, in a business, at home: it all counts.

Step 2 · Sort

Five questions. Stop at the first yes.

7 minutes

Same chat. Paste:

For each job ask me, in order: Can I stop it? Do it less often? Is it the same every time? Is it the same decision every time? Does it need me? Stop at my first yes and tell me what to do with that job.

What each "yes" means is further down.

Step 3 · Hand over

Turn your biggest job into steps

12 minutes · after the break

Same chat. Paste:

Take my biggest job. Ask me three questions about it. Then write it as numbered steps and mark the step that must be me.
Step 4 · Create your assistant

Give that job its own assistant

a. Still in the same chat, paste this. It writes the instructions for you, from everything you just told it:

Now write instructions for an AI assistant that will help me with this one job every week. Include: who I am, the job, the steps we just wrote, and the step that must stay with me. Tell it to use plain words, ask one question at a time, not suggest any tool until it understands the job, and, when I start a new chat, first ask me what I did about my last task. Write it as instructions to the assistant, under 200 words, so I can paste it into a Project.

b. Copy its answer: tap the small copy icon under the reply.
c. Open Projects, tap New project, name it after the job.
d. Paste the answer into the Project's Instructions, not into a chat. Save.

No Projects on your phone? Keep the instructions in your notes app and paste them at the top of every new chat. Gemini user? Save them as a Skill (see Gemini).

Step 5 · The Monday test

Did it remember you?

2 minutes

Start a new chat inside your new Project. Don't explain anything. Paste:

Help me plan this job for this week. Keep it realistic. End by asking which day I'll do it.

If it knows the job and your steps without being told, it worked.

Step 6 · Before you close it

Make sure Monday happens

On a paid ChatGPT or Claude plan? You can ask it to schedule the task to run automatically instead (limits apply).

Keep this

The five questions

Ask them in this order. Stop at the first yes.

  1. Can I stop it?A report nobody reads. Stop. It's free.
  2. Can I do it less often?Monthly instead of weekly.
  3. Is it the same every time?Reminders, follow-ups. This is where AI helps.
  4. Is it the same decision every time?Write the rule down once.
  5. Does it need me?An unhappy customer. Keep that one yourself.
Gemini users

Gemini calls it a Skill

Same instructions, but no folder. Save the instructions from step 4 as a Skill, then type / and its name in any chat. You need a personal Google account and Keep Activity switched on. If you don't see Skills yet, use ChatGPT or Claude today.

Three rules to keep

It drafts. You decide. Check any number, name, date or law before you use it.

Share how you work, not your secrets. No passwords, IC number, health details or TAC codes. Ever.

One task. Seven days. Not seven tasks in one day.
